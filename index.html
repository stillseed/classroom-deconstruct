The Foundation

What is Classroom
Deconstruct?

A deliberate slowdown — not to promote passivity, but to foster a deeper connection with our authentic selves. It disrupts ingrained patterns and reminds us of the importance of being real.

01

Unlearn

Let go of what was never truly yours. The inherited beliefs, the performed roles, the survival patterns that once protected you but now constrain you.

02

Unmask

Remove the false roles and reveal the raw self. The mask didn't protect you — it protected your image. Beneath it, you are still here.

03

Transform

Not by becoming someone else, but by returning to who you've always been. Stripping away false layers to reveal the authentic individual within.

The Architecture

The Five Pillars

These aren't steps to be followed, but invitations to embrace different kinds of honesty. You don't move through them once — you cycle through them your entire life.

LEAD
Listen · Engage · Awaken · Discern
+
The journey inward, before leading others.

LEAD begins with stillness. Not taking charge — but learning to listen. You'll awaken to what's been running you, and start choosing differently, from clarity rather than default.
"This is not about leading others first. It's about leading yourself — through presence, participation, self-awareness, and choice."

Core Practices

  • Stillness practice & body scan
  • Listening to resistance journaling
  • Pattern mirror & inherited belief mapping
  • Clarity compass & alignment reflection
  • One-sentence reflection circles
STAY
Settle · Track · Allow · Yield
+
The art of presence under pressure.

STAY teaches you to remain. With discomfort. With emotion. With the truth that just surfaced. Most of us escape the moment we feel something — this pillar trains us to stay.
"We settle not to escape reality — but to meet it from the ground."

Core Practices

  • Arrival breath & body check
  • Grounding scan & anchoring
  • Felt-sense body tracking
  • Emotional allowing without resistance
  • Surrender & yielding gestures
RISE
Root · Inspire · Serve · Embody
+
Reclaiming authenticity, vision, and purpose.

RISE isn't about climbing. It's about alignment — with your values, your fire, your service, and your embodiment. This is where vision meets action.
"Rooting is how we rise with clarity — not louder, but truer."

Core Practices

  • Values mapping & root identification
  • Memory drop-in for inspiration
  • Gift mapping & authentic service
  • Integrity gap mapping
  • Embodiment through consistent action
BURN
Break · Unmask · Reveal · Nourish
+
Dismantling what no longer serves.

BURN is about disruption. You'll break what's no longer true, unmask the roles you've played, reveal something raw — then nourish what remains. This isn't collapse. It's transformation.
"Breakage isn't failure — it's the beginning of something real."

Core Practices

  • False structure mapping & breaking
  • Mask mapping & unmasking circle
  • Truth threshold writing & reveal
  • Write & tear ritual
  • Self-tending & nourishment ritual
HOLD
Hear · Offer · Lean · Deepen
+
Becoming a safe space for others to heal and transform.

HOLD is where the work becomes real. You'll learn to truly listen, to offer space without rescuing, to lean when you're tired, and to deepen — not for intensity, but for integrity.
"To hold is to remain. To witness without rushing. To support without ego. To return — even when it would be easier to retreat."

Core Practices

  • Silent listening dyads
  • Role awareness mapping
  • Support mapping & trust lean
  • Depth vs. intensity mapping
  • Commitment to sustained presence
The Path

10-Week Facilitator
Training

A 10-week immersive training for individuals ready to lead with presence, truth, and depth. Not about teaching — about holding space, embodying truth, and facilitating transformation from the inside out.

01
Weeks 01 — 02

Foundation

Establishing the container. Understanding the philosophy, intention, and origins of Classroom Deconstruct. Building psychological safety, trauma sensitivity, and presence.

02
Weeks 03 — 07

Pillar Embodiment

Deep experiential immersion into each of the five pillars. Each week includes live sessions, peer-led practice, somatic awareness, and journaling integration.

03
Weeks 08 — 10

Mastery & Integration

Culminating in the final practicum — leading a 60-90 minute session with a real group, observed live and assessed using the CD Rubric. Closing with integration and certification.

The Experiences

Three Spaces
for Deep Work

Not courses. Not workshops. Containers — shaped to hold what emerges when people stop performing and start being honest.

The Deconstruction Lab
Monthly · 2 hours · 6–10 people
+
A monthly space for deep inquiry and inner architecture.

Trace the roots of your beliefs. Examine what still belongs. Loosen what no longer serves. This isn't debate — it's intellectual honesty in community. Inner work, in real time.

Energy Arc: Breathe → Trace → Reveal → Prompt → Reflect → Breathe again
"You don't need to leave with certainty. You just need to leave a little more honest."

Session Flow

  • Opening Grounding — 5 min breathwork
  • Session Frame — 5 min, state the belief
  • Individual Inquiry — 15-20 min journaling
  • Deconstruction Round — 60-70 min
  • Final Reflection — 10 min journaling
  • Closing Breath — 5 min
The Listening Lounge
2 hours · 6–10 people · Talking Object
+
A soft, structured space for honest inner reflection.

Where thoughts can breathe and stories can land without being fixed, rushed, or judged. Not therapy. Not debate. Not performance. Just space — with shape.

Energy Arc: Settle → Reflect → Share → Open → Close
"Speak if it's true — stay silent if it's not ready. Let the silence do some of the work."

Session Flow

  • Arrival + Grounding — 5 min stillness
  • Opening Frame — 3 min, state agreements
  • Journaling Prompt — 10-15 min
  • Sharing Round — 30-40 min with talking object
  • Open Dialogue — 30 min (optional, consent-based)
  • Closing Word + Meditation — 8-10 min
The Unmasking Circle
2 hours · 6–10 max · Sacred Space
+
A sacred space where masks fall and shared humanity softens shame.

Speak truths you've carried in silence — without being fixed, analyzed, or judged. No pressure. No performance. No advice. No comparison. Just truth — held, not handled.

Energy Arc: Settle → Unmask → Witness → Reflect → Integrate → Return
"What do you carry that no one sees? What truth wants to be spoken in this space?"

Session Flow

  • Opening Meditation — 5 min somatic arrival
  • Set the Frame — 5 min, name agreements
  • Optional Journaling — 5-10 min soft entry
  • Unmasking Round — 60-70 min
  • Reflection Round — 10 min
  • Closing Meditation — 3-5 min

"You are not here to teach. You are not the authority. You are here to hold the container — with presence, with steadiness, and with care."

  • You are a space holder, not a performer
  • You model presence, not perfection
  • You listen more than you speak
  • You trust the group's wisdom
  • You embody what you teach
  • Let the tools do the work. Let the space breathe.
The Standard

Certification
Criteria

Certification is not about performance. It's about embodiment. Demonstrating that you can hold space with presence, integrity, and care.

01
Complete All Modules
Attend every session. Participate in all peer practice rounds. Engage fully in journaling, group check-ins, and debriefs.
02
Weekly Reflections
Complete workbook reflections for each pillar. Show insight, integration, and awareness. Submit 1-page summary each week.
03
Final Practicum
Facilitate a 60-90 minute session embodying 1 or more CD pillars. Observed live and assessed using the CD Rubric (Pass: 22+/30).
04
Peer & Mentor Feedback
Openly receive group and facilitator feedback. Apply feedback where possible. Demonstrate growth and responsiveness.
05
5 Core Qualities
Presence, Integrity, Responsiveness, Container-Holding, Self-Awareness. Assessed across 6 dimensions on a 1-5 scale.
06
Final Reflection
"What I now hold, and how I will lead." May be written, audio, or video format. Submitted before closing ceremony.
